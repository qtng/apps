A seal of your own

Type Vietnamese with Telex, pick the Chữ Nôm, and the seal appears as you type, in the fonts of chunom.org.

  • Red characters in a frame or white characters cut out of a solid block, in several inks.
  • Square, rounded, oval or round.
  • Read in columns from the right or in rows, with worn edges like a real seal.
  • Download a PNG with a transparent background, or share it as a link.
